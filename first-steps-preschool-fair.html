Questions

Does it cost anything for families?

No. Families always get in free. RSVP so we can send you the exhibitor list and talk schedule ahead of time.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes. There's a supervised play area and activities so parents can talk with exhibitors.

Is the venue accessible?

Every Resource Commons venue is chosen for step-free access, accessible restrooms and nearby parking.

What if it snows?

Winter fairs have a published snow date. We'll email everyone who RSVPs if anything changes.
